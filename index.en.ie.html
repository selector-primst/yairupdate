③ Goods & Effective Storage Volume

📐 Calculated per GB 50072-2021 "Code for Design of Cold Store":
Storage tonnage = internal volume × overall factor η × food design density ρ ÷ 1000
· Overall factor η = volume tier factor (clause 3.0.3) × goods form factor × stacking factor, after deducting space for aisles, equipment, columns and pallets
· Goods form factor: bulk/bagged goods are irregular and need more clearance (0.85~0.90); boxed/palletized are regular (0.92~0.95)
· Food design density ρ is taken from Table 3.0.5, then reduced per the selected stacking method
· Changing goods type or stacking method recalculates effective volume and storage tonnage in real time (click Calculate to see the change)
· For rack storage, clause 3.0.4 allows using the sum of maximum storage per pallet position

💧 Water-to-Ice Cooling Load

Professional water-to-ice formula (three heat stages):
Q_cool = m × c_water × (t_water − 0) = m × 4.187 × t_water
Q_freeze (latent heat) = m × 334.9 kJ/kg (0℃ water → 0℃ ice)
Q_subcool = m × c_ice × (0 − t_ice) = m × 2.1 × |t_ice|
Total heat Q_total = Q_cool + Q_latent + Q_subcool (kJ)
Required cooling P(kW) = Q_total ÷ (3600 × t) × safety factor ÷ (daily run time/24)
